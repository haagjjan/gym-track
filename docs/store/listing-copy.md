## App Store description

```text
GymTrack is a strength-training log designed for use on your phone between sets.

What you can do:
- Log workouts with weight, reps, reps in reserve (RIR), set notes, and a built-in rest timer.
- Start from an empty workout, reuse saved templates, or browse the exercise library.
- Review past workouts in History and import or export your workout data as CSV.
- Track per-exercise progress over time with weight, estimated 1RM, and rep charts.
- View weekly muscle volume on an interactive 3D body map.
- Explore the app with a guided example that keeps your own training log separate.

Offline behavior:
GymTrack queues newly logged sets and exercises on your device when reception is poor and syncs them when you are back online. Editing or deleting existing entries requires an internet connection.

Account and privacy:
- No ads, no subscriptions, and no third-party analytics trackers.
- Export your account data or schedule account deletion directly from Settings.
- Available only to adults (18+) living in Switzerland.
```

## Google Play full description

```text
GymTrack is a strength-training log built for quick, one-handed use in the gym.

Core features:
- Live workout logger: record weight, reps, reps in reserve (RIR), and set notes, with an integrated rest timer.
- Templates and exercises: start an empty workout, reuse your own templates, or pick from the exercise catalog.
- History and data portability: browse past workouts and import or export workout logs as CSV.
- Lift progress: inspect weight, estimated 1RM, and rep trends for each exercise.
- 3D muscle volume: see how your weekly sets distribute across muscle groups on an interactive 3D figure.
- Guided demo: try the workflow with example data that never mixes with your real workouts.

Offline behavior:
If your gym has poor reception, GymTrack can queue newly logged sets and exercises on your device and sync them once a connection returns. Editing or deleting existing records requires an active connection.

Privacy and availability:
- Free to use, with no ads and no third-party analytics SDKs.
- Data export and account deletion are built into Settings.
- Offered exclusively to adults (18+) living in Switzerland.
```
