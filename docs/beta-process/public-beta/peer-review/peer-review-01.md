# Peer Review 01

This document lists the most important points that were noticed and criticized in a peer review after further adjustments of the beta release version of this project. The points are listed in an arbitrary order, with no additional meaning to the order.

Every point was reviewed in the Firefox virtualization of the mobile browser - resolution: 393 x 852

Date: 31.08.2026
SHA: 6e3c8b5e8f24d2ca004bfe60ed6ecdb66d15b2c4

1. The tutorial is not useful.
2. The user can get stuck in the information menus: Status Support & Service Status, Privacy Notice, Cookie & Device Storage Notice, Terms of Use, and Founding Beta Invitation.
3. The Firefox "Save Password" prompt appears multiple times at the wrong moments.
4. The accuracy of the muscles in the volume map is too low.
5. There are too few separate muscles shown in the volume map.
6. There are too few distinct muscles.
7. During the logging of a set, the user has no easy access to the recent progress of this exercise.
8. The dashboard widgets "Signed in as" and "Body Profile" contain similar informations
9. The 3D environment is a completely cut-off square.
10. The 3D environment is simple and uninteresting.
11. The podium is uninteresting and has many vertices.
12. There is only one 3D character.
13. The exercise reordering in the live workout menu can drag the exercise cards down in a buggy manner.
14. The exercise reordering in the live workout menu can drag an exercise further to the left and right without bounds.
15. The user can accidentally zoom in by double-clicking.
16. In the live exercise logging, the session name and time are not aligned with the other buttons.
17. The entered BFP is the standard field taken as the autofilled Firefox password name.
