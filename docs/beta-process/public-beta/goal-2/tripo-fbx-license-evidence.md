# Tripo FBX Licence Evidence

**Decision date:** 2026-08-19

**Conclusion:** The two bundled FBX models may remain in the public beta and public repository
under Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (`CC BY 4.0`), provided the attribution and
modification notice below remain with the delivered application and repository.

workspace, the humanoid model and its public visibility. The screenshot is controller evidence;
it is not committed because it contains unrelated personal browser chrome and account context.

## Required attribution

The following notice is carried in the public Support page and repository `NOTICE`:

> The 3D avatar and pedestal models were generated with Tripo 3D and are used under
> CC BY 4.0. They were modified for this application through mesh separation, transforms,
> materials, shaders and rendering integration. Tripo does not endorse this project.

The application must keep links to Tripo 3D and `CC BY 4.0` with that notice. `NOTICE` must remain
clear that the repository's MIT licence does not apply to the FBX files. If an original public
asset URL or creator designation becomes available, add it to both attribution surfaces.

## Residual boundary

`CC BY 4.0` supplies the redistribution and adaptation permission required for delivery; it does
not provide a warranty of non-infringement or license unrelated trademark, privacy, publicity or
personality rights. The models depict a generic fictional humanoid and pedestal rather than an
identified person or third-party mark. Any future replacement, substantially different asset, or
new Tripo generation requires its own plan-and-licence record.
