## P0: fix on `main` now, outside the rework

**Signup records age and terms consent that the user never gave.** The signup form sets `noValidate`, and its submit handler never reads the two checkboxes. It always sends `adultAttested: true` plus the current terms and privacy versions (`apps/web/src/features/auth/auth-screen.tsx:68-74`, `:119`). I reproduced this on 2026-11-08: with both boxes unticked, the browser sent the full attestation, and the request was intercepted before it reached the API. The API trusts the flag (`z.literal(true)`), so accounts created through the web form store `adult_attested_at` and `policy_accepted_at` regardless of the boxes. This is live, because production has open signup (ADR 0022).

- Fix: block submit until both boxes are checked. Leave the API payload and its validation unchanged (no ADR needed). Add a test that submits with the boxes unticked.
- Follow-up: decide whether accounts created since open signup need to confirm their consent again.

## 1. UX and visual (Step 5)

The screens work. The main problems are that they look and behave like a sci-fi website, not a phone app, and that the app frame breaks inside the real WebViews.

### App frame inside the phone app (P1, known since Step 3)
- On iOS, the top bar sits under the status bar and the Dynamic Island. On Android, white bands sit above and below the app. Confirmed cause on iOS: no `viewport-fit=cover` in `app/layout.tsx:26` and no top inset on `MobileTopBar` (`features/shell/app-shell.tsx:199`). On Android the cause is probably the same: Capacitor's SystemBars docs describe padded bars when `viewport-fit` isn't `cover`. Confirm that in Step 9 together with the native bar settings. Only the real WebView shows this; Safari and Chrome hide it.
- Guided example, step 6 (Volume): the bay's radial glow covers the guide card, and the figure is pushed into the tab bar. Reported by the owner; reproduced at 390 px (`baseline…/shots/demo-guided-390/step-06.png`).

### Doesn't work in the app (P1, confirmed by Sol)
- **File downloads**: the account data export in Settings (`features/settings/privacy-data-panel.tsx:25`, which also reports success either way) and the workout CSV export (`features/history/csv-panel.tsx:102`) both use browser downloads. A Capacitor WebView doesn't download blobs without native code. Fix both with a native share or filesystem plugin, in the mobile work. An emailed export would change the API, so it needs its own decision.
- **Terms and Privacy links on signup** open with `target="_blank"` (`auth-screen.tsx:142`). In the app that leaves the WebView or does nothing. Use an in-app sheet or route.

### Navigation and back (P2)
- Legal and support pages always go "back" to `/settings`, even when opened from Help or signup (`features/legal/legal-page.tsx:6`). iOS has no swipe-back in the app, so every screen below a tab needs a back control that follows the real history.
- Help, Messages and Settings have no back control. The tab bar still works, so these are P3, not dead ends (Sol rejected the "trapped" claim).
- The History tab holds three different areas: workout history, templates and the exercise catalog. Templates highlights the History tab.
- Dashboard "See all workouts" goes to `/workout` (the launcher), not to `/workouts` (history) (`features/dashboard/dashboard-screen.tsx:225`).
- In history, tapping the workout in progress expands a row, and resuming takes a second tap (`features/history/history-row.tsx:28`).

### Primary actions and thumb reach (P2)
- In a live workout, Finish sits at the top right (`features/session/session-header.tsx:87`), out of thumb reach. The Hevy/Strong pattern puts it in a bottom bar.
- The dashboard has two identical "Resume workout" actions (the big button and the centre tab), and in-page links duplicate the Progress and Volume tabs.
- "+ New" in history and exercises sits in the top-right header.
- While a workout is in progress, the launcher hides templates and quick start. Discarding is only possible from inside the session.

### Touch targets (P2, measured)
The scan found elements under 44 px on 15 of 31 routes at 390 px. The worst:
- `/progress`: 8 (chart mode chips at 32 px, time-window buttons)
- `/support`: 7
- legal pages: 4 each
- `/signup`: 5 (16 px checkboxes and inline links)
- `/workouts/templates`: Edit, Duplicate and Delete at 32 px
- `/weekly-volume`: Front and Back at 34 px
- `/settings`: the storage info button at 28 px

Inline text links ("Forgot your password?", "See all workouts") are about 14–16 px tall throughout.

### Forms and controls (P2)
- Password fields have no show/hide toggle.
- Settings toggles look like text buttons reading "ON"/"OFF". They already have `role="switch"` and `aria-checked`, so the fix is visual only.
- Settings mixes two save patterns: toggles save immediately, while body profile and heat ceiling need a Save button.
- Body profile fields are text inputs with the unit inside the field, so the phone doesn't show a numeric keyboard.
- Merge exercises in Settings shows `MERGE_AWAY (duplicate)`, `MERGE_INTO (keep)` and `REVIEW_MERGE` (`features/settings/merge-exercises-panel.tsx:43-45`).

### States (P2)
- Progress opens on a 1-month window. For a user with older data, that shows two empty states. The 3M and MAX buttons widen it with one tap, but the empty state itself offers no action.
- Weekly volume shows three empty panels at once, with no "Log a workout" action, and the unlit figure is nearly invisible on the dark background.
- `/cancel-deletion` without a token shows a disabled button and no explanation (P2 per Sol).
- A completed workout still says "Tap an exercise to log sets".

### Visual system (P3 each, but it is the main theme)
Every screen group reported the same set of generic-AI and terminal patterns:
- monospace body text
- tracked ALL-CAPS eyebrows above every panel
- `//` and `·` separators
- `01`/`02` numbering on lists that aren't sequences
- square bullet glyphs
- chamfered, glowing cards nested inside cards
- "→" on most links
- decorative connector beams on the dashboard

Each one is minor, but together they read as a cockpit, not a fitness app. The new DESIGN.md (Step 8) has to replace this system, not patch it. See the root causes under Copy.

### Wider screens (P3)
At 768 and 1440 px, most screens keep a narrow centred column with large empty areas. The phone is the main target, so this ranks after everything above.

### Worth keeping
The 3D figure and the bay on the dashboard and volume screens render well in both WebViews. The session logger's exercise list and the progress chart are clear. The tab bar with the centre workout button already works as a phone pattern.

### Accessibility
axe found no contrast violations. It found:
- `aria-required-children` on the session exercise list (the sortable list has no list items; `shared/ui/sortable-list.tsx:104`)
- `definition-list` in the dashboard identity card
- `page-has-heading-one` on the session screens

## 2. Copy (Step 6)

The audit extracted 1,661 strings and flagged 225:

| Category | Count |
|---|---|
| CASE | 69 |

| TERMINAL | 41 |
| JARGON | 30 |
| INCONSISTENT | 15 |
| TONE | 13 |

**Root causes**, which have to be fixed in the design system and not string by string:
- The body font is monospace for the whole app (`app/globals.css:76`, `--font-mono` on `body`).
- CSS forces 50 `uppercase` and 70 `tracking-*` uses across 26 files. Many labels are written normally but shown in caps.
- Enum values and internal keys are shown to users, for example `WORKOUT_COMPLETE`, `EXPORT_CSV`, `ACCOUNT_RECOVERY`, `RETURN_TO_LOGIN`, `NO_RECORDED_SETS`, `HEAT_STAGE_X`, `EST_1RM`, and the demo titles `EXAMPLE_FULL_BODY_8`.

**Most common text problems:**
1. Errors that don't say what to do next ("That did not work", "The session could not be started.", "Something went wrong").
2. Raw validation messages, such as zod's "String must contain at least 1 character(s)" on login.
3. Jargon without explanation (RIR, est. 1RM, "heat ceiling", "functional storage").
4. The same thing under different names ("Discard workout" and "Delete workout log", "session" and "workout").

The appendix lists every flagged string with a suggested rewrite. Rewrite each screen's copy in Step 10 together with its layout, following the voice guide in the new DESIGN.md.
